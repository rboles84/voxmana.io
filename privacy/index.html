Vox Mana Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Vox Mana keeps in your browser, what leaves it when you use connected features, how that information is used, and the choices you have.

Effective September 13, 2026
Plain-English Summary

What this page is saying

Vox Mana keeps some information in your browser and sends limited information outside it when you use connected features. The sections below explain those information flows, their purposes and retention, and your available controls.

01

Information Vox Mana Uses

The following information is used or processed when you choose the related features:

  • Your Archscry answers, selected preferences, and resulting Commander identity or placement reading are used in your browser. The latest completed reading is stored there so its dossier can return on the same browser and device.
  • Your Reading Finds, Sparks, and Anchors are stored in your browser as a local companion to card discovery.
  • When you use card search, card details, or card imagery, the relevant search text or card name is sent outside your browser.
  • Limited product analytics use structured reading-progress and result information, plus bounded Field Guide activity. They do not include reading prose, free-form card searches, saved readings, names, email addresses, or feedback messages.
  • If you send page feedback, the message, optional reply email, and page context such as the path, visible section, simplified browser/device label, viewport size, and timestamp are delivered through a feedback service.
  • Hosting and security providers may process ordinary request metadata and technical logs needed to deliver and protect the site.
02

How Vox Mana Uses Information

Vox Mana uses this information to:

  • generate and display your Commander identity reading in the browser;
  • restore your latest saved result on the same browser and device;
  • keep Reading Finds available as you explore cards;
  • retrieve Magic card data and imagery;
  • understand whether players can complete an Archscry reading and use the Field Guide;
  • receive and respond to optional page feedback;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and protect the service from abuse.
03

Services That Help Vox Mana Work

Vox Mana uses outside services for specific purposes:

  • Scryfall provides card search, card details, and imagery. Search requests and card names are sent to Scryfall when those features are used.
  • Hosting and security services deliver the site and help protect it from abuse.
  • A feedback service delivers optional messages to the Vox Mana feedback inbox.
  • A product analytics service receives the limited Archscry and Field Guide activity described above.
  • Other sites receive ordinary request information if you choose to follow an outbound link.

Those providers handle information under their own privacy notices and practices.

04

Sharing and No Sale

Vox Mana does not sell personal information or build advertising profiles. Information leaves your browser only for the purposes and recipients described above, when required by law, or when necessary to respond to abuse.

05

Retention

Saved readings and Reading Finds remain in your browser until you clear them, clear browser storage, or the browser removes its stored data. They are not copied to a Vox Mana account or synchronized between devices. Information handled by Scryfall and service providers is retained under their own policies and the service settings Vox Mana uses. Feedback may remain in the feedback inbox so it can be reviewed and answered; operational and security logs may be kept as needed to operate and protect the site.

06

Your Choices

  • You can retake a reading and replace a prior saved result.
  • You can forget the saved reading from its dossier.
  • You can clear Reading Finds separately from the Maze.
  • You can clear all Vox Mana data stored on this device through your browser settings.
  • Feedback is optional, and you can leave the reply-email field blank.
07

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or Vox Mana's information practices may be submitted through the site's Feedback button or the Vox Mana public source repository.
