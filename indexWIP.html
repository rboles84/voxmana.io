VOX MANA

Find your placeShape your play

Commander is easier to explore when you can name what you enjoy. Vox Mana gives you a place to work that out, search for cards without memorizing Scryfall syntax, and learn from the games you play.

Author’s note

I built Vox Mana because I suck at Commander. I also wanted to help my son and other players by putting useful information at their fingertips. I’m still learning from and refining this project, and I’d love feedback on the flow and visuals.
