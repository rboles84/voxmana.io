I // Archscry · Commander direction

See what your play choices point toward.

Archscry asks about Commander table moments: what you protect, rebuild, pressure, or let unfold. Your answers support a direction, not a personality verdict.

Its dossier lets you inspect why that direction appeared, test the fit, see how it plays, browse Commander starting points, and carry discovery threads into the Maze.

The question it answersWhat kind of Commander direction sounds like me?

Start an Archscry reading →
II // The Implicit Maze · Card discovery

Turn an idea into a searchable path.

The Implicit Maze can translate plain language, accept exact Scryfall syntax through the Operator's Hand, or build a query visually in the Loom.

You can arrive with a dossier thread or begin with a fresh idea. Reading Finds keeps useful cards together locally so they can return to the reading that started the search.

The question it answersWhat cards express or explore this direction?

Enter the Implicit Maze →
III // Strategium · Table literacy

Learn the table around the deck.

Strategium helps you read Commander moments, set expectations, and choose one clearer next step. Its guidance offers possible explanations, not judgments about a player or pod.

Start with the moment you are in, or use the Commander Console to study recurring table signals in more depth.
