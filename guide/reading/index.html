Field Guide // Reading & Dossier

Read the result. Choose one next step.

Your result is a direction to inspect, not a verdict to obey.

Use the dossier for the question you have now. You never need to study every section before acting.

I // What placement means

A supported Commander direction.

Archscry compares the signals in your recorded answers and shows the direction they support most strongly. It is not a personality label, a power ranking, or a claim that only one identity can fit you.

If another direction is shown, it is there because the same answers also support a useful comparison. The dossier helps you test both the fit and its limits.

II // Where to start

Begin with your question, not the first tab.

Choose one lane. The other sections can wait.

I want to understand it Placement + Why This Fits

See what your answers supported, then test whether the direction sounds true in play.

I want to build from it Start Here + Commander Browsing Starts

Choose a deck direction, then compare commanders, precons, and public deck examples.

I want to keep exploring Card Signals + Mana Notes + Maze Discovery

Inspect example cards and lands, or carry the direction into a live search.
