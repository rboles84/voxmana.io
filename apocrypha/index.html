How These Sources Are Used

Use the source that fits the question.

Official design articles explain design intent. Official stories and plane pages establish lore and setting. Official archives preserve older context. One source type cannot stand in for another.

Supplemental references can help you follow a trail, but they do not establish official claims. A checked link means only that the URL worked on the recorded date.

Rules and card-record sources are not included yet. Vox Mana is not a rules engine, legality checker, deckbuilder, wiki, or purchasing guide.
