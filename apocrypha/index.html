The Apocrypha

Where Vox Mana shows its work.

Apocrypha lists the public sources behind Vox Mana's color, lore, rules, and Commander identity work. Each source card should say what the source is, what it can support, and where its authority stops.

Official sources can support design, lore, rules, card-record, or archive claims according to their source type. Supplemental references can help with navigation and context, but they do not carry official claims by themselves.

How These Sources Are Used

Authority comes from source type.

Vox Mana is not an official Magic source, rules engine, legality checker, deckbuilder, wiki, or purchasing guide. Apocrypha shows the public evidence Vox Mana uses and the limits on that evidence.

Official Sources

Support only matching claims.

Publisher authority does not turn a design article into rules text or a lore article into card-record truth.

Link Checks

Verification is separate.

Links checked by GET show a checked-link badge. Other links remain pending and should not be described as verified.

Supplemental References

Useful, but limited.

Supplemental references stay visible because they can help readers follow a trail. They are separated from official evidence because they cannot carry Vox Mana's official claims by themselves.

Known Gaps

Partial coverage stays honest.

Known source gaps remain. Vox Mana should show partial coverage honestly rather than imply a complete official library.
